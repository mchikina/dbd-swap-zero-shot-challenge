# Zero-shot prediction of DNA-binding-domain swap binding profiles

ChEC-seq binding profiles for whole-genome-duplicated transcription factor (TF) paralog pairs in
*S. cerevisiae*, from GEO series GSE179430 (Gera, Jonas, More, Barkai, *eLife* 2022,
doi:10.7554/eLife.73225). For nine paralog pairs the authors profiled, in addition to the two
wild-type TFs, chimeras in which the DNA-binding domain (DBD) of one paralog was replaced by the
other's. Seven pairs have the complete set of four constructs.

## The challenge

For a paralog pair (A, B) with all four constructs measured:

| role | construct | genotype pattern |
|---|---|---|
| A | wild-type A, MNase-tagged | `A::A-Mnase` |
| B | wild-type B, MNase-tagged | `B::B-Mnase` |
| A_B_DBD | A body carrying B's DBD | `A::A-BDBD-Mnase` |
| B_A_DBD | B body carrying A's DBD | `B::B-ADBD-Mnase` |

predict the genome-wide binding profiles of `A_B_DBD` and `B_A_DBD` from the wild-type profiles of A
and B (and any other information that is not a DBD-swap measurement), without training on any
DBD-swap track. Targets are the replicate-mean tracks in `analysis/targets/mean_tracks/`.

Evaluation (as computed in `analysis/targets/mean_track_correlation.tsv`): Pearson r between the
predicted and measured mean track on log2(CPM per 100 bp + 1), genome-wide (chrM excluded) and at
peaks (union of the top 1000 bins of the set's 4 mean tracks). The rows `A vs A_B_DBD`, `B vs A_B_DBD`,
`A vs B_A_DBD`, `B vs B_A_DBD` in the tables below are the naive baselines "predict the swap = one of
the wild-type profiles".

### Foreground and background sets

Foreground = sets in which all six cross-peak correlations between the 4 mean tracks exceed 0.2:
**Dot6/Tod6, Pdr8/Yrr1, Yhp1/Yox1** (12 experiments, 31 replicates). The other four complete sets
(Ecm22/Upc2, Fkh1/Fkh2, Gis1/Rph1, Vhr1/Vhr2) are provided as background. Ace2/Swi5 and Cup9/Tos8
have only one swap direction in GEO and are not included. Flag: `all_peak_r_above_0.2` in
`analysis/targets/manifest.tsv`.

## Data

- Source: GSE179430, 348 samples, 138 experiments (wild-type TFs, paralog deletions, *K. lactis*
  orthologs, DBD swaps). Details and sample naming: `GSE179430/README.md`; per-sample genotype,
  strain and instrument: `GSE179430/metadata/samples.tsv`.
- Processed data as deposited: one file per sample, one integer per genomic base (read 5' ends,
  i.e. MNase cut sites), 12,157,105 lines, chrI..chrXVI then chrM, assembly S288C R64 / sacCer3.
- Genome: `genome/` (chromosome sizes and FASTA index tracked; FASTA via `scripts/download_data.sh`).
- Mean tracks (`analysis/targets/mean_tracks/<expname>.mean_cpm.npy`, not tracked, regenerated by
  `scripts/run_pipeline.sh`): float32, per-base CPM, each replicate normalised to its own depth then
  averaged with equal weight. `analysis/targets/manifest.tsv` lists set, role, replicate files and depths.
- Sequencing depth ranges from 25k to 33M reads per sample (`analysis/peaks/sample_totals.tsv`).
  Several swap and wild-type experiments include a replicate below 1M reads.
- Not included: raw reads (SRA SRP326882), DBD boundary coordinates of the chimeras (see the paper's
  methods and supplementary tables).

## Reproduce

```
scripts/download_data.sh    # GEO tar (470 MB) + sacCer3 FASTA
scripts/run_pipeline.sh     # uv venv, bins, peak figures, mean tracks, correlation tables
```

## Repository layout

```
GSE179430/            README, metadata (samples.tsv, GEO SOFT, series matrices); raw/ is downloaded
genome/               sacCer3 chrom.sizes (UCSC order and GEO concatenation order), FASTA index
analysis/peaks/       01-04: 100 bp binning, selection of 10 peak regions, figures of all 138 experiments
analysis/targets/     05-06: mean tracks, replicate and cross-experiment correlations, manifest, tables
scripts/              download_data.sh, run_pipeline.sh
```

Figures of all 138 experiments at 10 peak regions: `analysis/peaks/fig_heatmap_10regions.png`,
`analysis/peaks/fig_lines_10regions.png` (method in `analysis/peaks/README.md`).
